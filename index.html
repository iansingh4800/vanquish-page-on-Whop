Which plan should I start with?

If you want the fastest route, Rapid Options has the lowest price, no consistency rule and no minimum trades, so you can pass in one day. If you trade spreads or want an end-of-day drawdown, pick Advanced Options. Options is the classic middle ground.

Can I trade Nour's alerts on a Vanquish account?

Yes. Options and Rapid Options allow long calls and puts (single-leg). Advanced Options adds level 4: spreads, multi-leg and selling to open. All positions must close by 3:59pm ET, with no overnight holds.

How do payouts work?

After you pass, you move to a Performance account with no profit target. Once you're above the $250 minimum you can request a payout: any trading day on Options & Rapid, every 4 trading days on Advanced.
